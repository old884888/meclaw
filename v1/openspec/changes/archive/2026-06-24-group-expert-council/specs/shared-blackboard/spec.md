## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Structured shared blackboard

A council run SHALL maintain a single structured shared blackboard with the fields: `Current_Consensus`, `Open_Divergences`, `Falsified_Hypotheses`, `Key_Constraints`, and `Context_Version`.

#### Scenario: Blackboard initialized at run start

- **WHEN** a council run begins
- **THEN** the system SHALL create a blackboard with empty lists and `Context_Version` = 1

#### Scenario: Blackboard fields populated across rounds

- **WHEN** a round completes
- **THEN** the blackboard SHALL be updated with newly agreed facts, open divergences, falsified hypotheses, and hard constraints discovered in that round

### Requirement: Append-not-overwrite update discipline

The blackboard SHALL be updated by appending or amending structured fields, and SHALL NOT be regenerated by asking a model to rewrite the entire blackboard from scratch. `Context_Version` SHALL increment by 1 on each update.

#### Scenario: Version increments on update

- **WHEN** the blackboard is updated after a round
- **THEN** `Context_Version` SHALL increase by exactly 1
- **AND** prior `Current_Consensus` entries SHALL NOT be silently dropped by a model rewrite

#### Scenario: Earlier conclusions preserved

- **WHEN** a later round adds new consensus items
- **THEN** earlier consensus items SHALL remain unless explicitly moved to `Falsified_Hypotheses` with a recorded reason

### Requirement: Blackboard as round context source

From Round 2 onward, Bots SHALL receive the current blackboard snapshot plus the current round's specific inputs as context, instead of the full prior transcript.

#### Scenario: Later rounds read snapshot not full history

- **WHEN** a Bot acts in Round 2, 3, or 4
- **THEN** its prompt SHALL include the latest blackboard snapshot and the round-specific inputs
- **AND** SHALL NOT include the entire verbatim transcript of all prior rounds

#### Scenario: Citation of blackboard version

- **WHEN** a Bot produces an output that relies on prior consensus
- **THEN** the output SHALL be able to cite the blackboard `Context_Version` it relied on

### Requirement: Dispute freeze escalation

When two Bots remain in conflict on the same point beyond a configured number of rounds, the system SHALL escalate the point as a frozen dispute for arbitration.

#### Scenario: Conflict escalated after threshold

- **WHEN** the same divergence persists in `Open_Divergences` beyond the freeze threshold
- **THEN** the system SHALL mark it as an escalated dispute
- **AND** SHALL surface it to human arbitration rather than spending further rounds on it
